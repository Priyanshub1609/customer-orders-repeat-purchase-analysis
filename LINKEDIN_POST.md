I’ve been working on a hands-on SQL portfolio project: **Customer Orders & Repeat Purchase Analysis**.

The objective is to understand customer purchasing behavior and uncover ways an e-commerce business could encourage repeat purchases.

**Analysis areas**
- One-time vs repeat customer segmentation
- Delivered revenue and repeat-customer revenue contribution
- Top customers by revenue
- City, acquisition-channel and product-category performance
- Monthly order trends
- Time between delivered purchases
- Data-quality checks for duplicate IDs and unmatched customer references

**Tools:** SQL Server, SSMS, JOINs, CTEs, GROUP BY, HAVING, CASE, LAG(), RANK(), and date calculations.

The dataset is synthetic and was created for learning and portfolio practice. I will share verified findings and business recommendations after reviewing the query results.

Project repository: https://github.com/Priyanshub1609/customer-orders-repeat-purchase-analysis
